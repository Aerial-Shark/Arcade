A moonlit merge puzzle

Hex Bloom

Plant seeds in the night garden. When three or more alike touch, they bloom into the next stage on the cell you just planted.

    • Point at a cell to preview what will bloom. Blooms can chain, and bigger groups and chains score more.
    • Thorny weeds creep in and block cells. A bloom right next to a weed makes it wither away.
    • Moon dew is rare: it becomes whatever blooms best where you plant it. Use Hold to save a seed for later.

    Three star blooms together clear for a huge bonus. The garden is lost when every cell is taken.

    Tap or click a cell · Arrows/WASD + Space · H hold · M mute
