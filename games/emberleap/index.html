A night rooftop run

Ember Leap

The city is asleep. Keep your lantern burning and run the roofs as far as the light will carry you.

  • Hold anywhere, or Space, to jump. Hold longer to leap higher.
  • Embers refuel your lantern and store up to 3 charges. Press again in mid-air to spend one on a double jump.
  • Bounce off washing lines. Loose tiles crumble, so keep moving. Chimneys knock your light down.
  • Fall between roofs, or let the lantern go out, and the run ends.

Paused

Tap, click or press Space to keep running.
