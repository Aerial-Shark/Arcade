A one-button orbit game

Perihelion

You circle a planet. Tap to let go and fly off along your orbit. Every planet crumbles while you hold on, so keep climbing.

  • Solid ground
  • Heavy: bends your flight
  • Twisted: flips your spin
  • Asteroid: don't touch

Land on new planets quickly to grow your chain multiplier. Circle one for more than 2 seconds and the chain breaks. The dotted line shows where you'll go, and it gets shorter the higher you climb.

Tap, click, or press Space
